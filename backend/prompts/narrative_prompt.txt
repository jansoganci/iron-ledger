This appears to be an annual prepayment booked in one month instead of being spread. Recommended action: create a prepaid asset and amortize over the contract period."
   Template (REVENUE lump): "The [account] GL shows a lump-sum of [GL amount] where the monthly amount should be [implied_monthly from hints].
